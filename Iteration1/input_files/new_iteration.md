- Justification: The current results provide a strong theoretical foundation but suffer from a "pre-asymptotic" diagnostic gap. The current analysis relies on global MSD regressions and characteristic function fits that are dominated by the Gaussian bulk, masking the underlying Lévy-stable physics. The proposed changes—specifically shifting to tail-index estimation (Hill estimator/MLE) and performing a local scaling analysis of velocity correlations—are high-ROI technical improvements. These changes directly address the "Reviewer Confidence" criteria by transforming a "negative" result (failure to observe anomalous diffusion) into a "mechanistic" result (quantifying the crossover from Gaussian to Lévy behavior). This shift from a descriptive report to a diagnostic study significantly increases the "Publishability Delta" without requiring new, expensive simulations. The Lorenz-96 analysis, in particular, offers a unique opportunity to demonstrate the universality of the $\alpha = 2/\xi$ relation in deterministic systems, which would elevate the paper's impact from a simulation study to a fundamental physical theory contribution.
